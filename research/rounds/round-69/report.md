## Frozen lexical warning controls

Accept-all, reject-all, content-overlap (less than 55% of non-stopword content words from source), exact 4-gram source overlap, novelty of numbers/proper names, and union of content overlap with name/number novelty. A warning applies to the full deterministic response sentence. Only source text and response text are used for prediction; annotation labels are used only afterward to score.

The highest-recall union wrongly flags 590/630 apparently clean responses, while content overlap still falsely warns on 367/630. Repeating exact source bytes or citing them does not solve semantic entailment. These checks are highly sensitive to paraphrase and benign novelty; they cannot be called reliable hallucination verifiers.

Removing the human-annotated implicit_true positive spans produces 114 positive responses rather than 138. Content overlap has 99 true positives, 389 false positives, 15 false negatives and F1 0.32890 under this stricter target. Distinguishing a source-unsupported claim from an actually false claim matters.

## Limits and next test

Human RAGTruth annotations concern outputs generated by other models. CDG did not produce those outputs or reduce their hallucinations. No causal citation provenance, full NLI, source authority or temporal validity was evaluated. This is a separate dataset family from HotpotQA, although QA is only one of RAGTruth's tasks. Source-specific test grouping prevents naive response-level split leakage. Compact JSON contains character-level and response-level confusion matrices, but never reproduces original corpus text.

Round 70 fixes lexical threshold calibration using the original training split and evaluates previously unexamined test source groups. Round 71 tests the mathematical source-only classification limit and lossless source-byte receipts.
